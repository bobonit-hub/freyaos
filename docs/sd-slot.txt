SD card slot
============

Freya talks to the card in SPI mode on SPI1. The six wires are the same
pins on the Blue Pill and the Black Pill. The headers are not, so each
board has its own drawing below. Power the slot from the board's 3.3 V
pin and join the two grounds. The card runs at 3.3 V on that pin.

    Signal     Pin    microSD     full-size SD
    ---------  -----  ----------  ------------
    CS         PA4    2  DAT3     1  CD/DAT3
    DI / MOSI  PA7    3  CMD      2  CMD
    VDD        3.3 V  4  VDD      4  VDD
    CLK / SCK  PA5    5  CLK      5  CLK
    GND        GND    6  VSS      3  VSS1 and 6 VSS2
    DO / MISO  PA6    7  DAT0     7  DAT0

DAT1 and DAT2 stay unused. Pull each up to 3.3 V with 10 kΩ: microSD
pins 8 and 1, or full-size pins 8 and 9. Freya pulls DO up inside the
chip. CS is driven and sits high while the card is idle. A card-detect
switch on the socket is left open.

A module that already labels CS, CLK, DO, DI, 3.3 V and GND takes the
same six wires. Its VCC goes to 3.3 V.


Blue Pill
---------

STM32F103C8T6, component side, USB connector at the top. The slot uses
the left header. Pin 1 of that header is VB, next to the USB socket.

                         micro USB
                    left
               VB  o
              C13  o
              C14  o
              C15  o
               A0  o
               A1  o
               A2  o
               A3  o
               A4  o-------- CS    DAT3
               A5  o-------- CLK
               A6  o-------- DO    DAT0
               A7  o-------- DI    CMD
               B0  o
               B1  o
              B10  o
              B11  o
                R  o
              3.3  o-------- VDD
                G  o-------- VSS
                G  o-------- VSS   (full-size pin 6; either ground)


Black Pill
----------

WeAct STM32F411CEU6, component side, USB-C connector at the top. The
slot uses the left header. Pin 1 of that header is 5V, next to the
USB-C socket. The 5V pin is not part of this wiring.

                         USB-C
                    left
               5V  o
                G  o-------- VSS
              3.3  o-------- VDD
              B10  o
               B2  o
               B1  o
               B0  o
               A7  o-------- DI    CMD
               A6  o-------- DO    DAT0
               A5  o-------- CLK
               A4  o-------- CS    DAT3
               A3  o
               A2  o
               A1  o
               A0  o
                R  o
              C15  o
              C14  o
              C13  o
               VB  o

The SOP-8 footprint on the back is on this same SPI bus: CS, CLK and
DI are PA4, PA5 and PA7, and DO is PA6 or PB4 depending on the board
revision. Leave that footprint empty so the card is the only device
on PA4.
