SD card slot
============

Freya talks to the card in SPI mode on SPI1. The six signal wires are the
same pins on the Blue Pill and the Black Pill. The headers are not, so each
board has its own drawing below. Join the two grounds. The card runs at
3.3 V, taken from a high-side switch rather than straight from the 3.3 V
pin: that switch is what `power sd off` opens.

    Signal     Pin    microSD     full-size SD
    ---------  -----  ----------  ------------
    CS         PA4    2  DAT3     1  CD/DAT3
    DI / MOSI  PA7    3  CMD      2  CMD
    VDD        switch  4  VDD      4  VDD     (PA8 gates it; see below)
    CLK / SCK  PA5    5  CLK      5  CLK
    GND        GND    6  VSS      3  VSS1 and 6 VSS2
    DO / MISO  PA6    7  DAT0     7  DAT0

PA8 drives the gate of a P-channel MOSFET. The source is the board's
3.3 V pin, the drain is the socket's VDD, and a 100 kΩ pull-down from
the gate to ground holds the card on while PA8 is still an input after
reset. PA8 low keeps the card on; PA8 high turns it off. PA8 is on the
right header of both boards. A socket tied straight to 3.3 V still
enumerates; `power sd off` then releases the bus and the filesystem,
and VDD stays up. Leave the socket off for a few hundred milliseconds
when the card has to come back from a real power-on reset.

DAT1 and DAT2 stay unused. Pull each up to 3.3 V with 10 kΩ: microSD
pins 8 and 1, or full-size pins 8 and 9. Freya pulls DO up inside the
chip. CS is driven and sits high while the card is idle. A card-detect
switch on the socket is left open.

A module that already labels CS, CLK, DO, DI, VCC and GND takes the
same six signal wires. Its VCC goes to the switched rail, not to the
board's 3.3 V pin.


Blue Pill
---------

STM32F103C8T6, component side, USB connector at the top. The slot uses
the left header. Pin 1 of that header is VB, next to the USB socket.

                         micro USB
                    left
               VB  o
              C13  o
              C14  o
              C15  o
               A0  o
               A1  o
               A2  o
               A3  o
               A4  o-------- CS    DAT3
               A5  o-------- CLK
               A6  o-------- DO    DAT0
               A7  o-------- DI    CMD
               B0  o
               B1  o
              B10  o
              B11  o
                R  o
              3.3  o-------- VDD   (the switch; gate is PA8)
                G  o-------- VSS
                G  o-------- VSS   (full-size pin 6; either ground)


Black Pill
----------

WeAct STM32F411CEU6, component side, USB-C connector at the top. The
slot uses the left header. Pin 1 of that header is 5V, next to the
USB-C socket. The 5V pin is not part of this wiring.

                         USB-C
                    left
               5V  o
                G  o-------- VSS
              3.3  o-------- VDD   (the switch; gate is PA8)
              B10  o
               B2  o
               B1  o
               B0  o
               A7  o-------- DI    CMD
               A6  o-------- DO    DAT0
               A5  o-------- CLK
               A4  o-------- CS    DAT3
               A3  o
               A2  o
               A1  o
               A0  o
                R  o
              C15  o
              C14  o
              C13  o
               VB  o

The SOP-8 footprint on the back is on this same SPI bus: CS, CLK and
DI are PA4, PA5 and PA7, and DO is PA6 or PB4 depending on the board
revision. A chip soldered there is a LittleFS volume, mounted at
/spi1. A blank chip is formatted the first time it is mounted. It uses
the card's chip select, so the socket has to be empty.
With no chip fitted the bus belongs to the card, the same as before.
